About Me

Hello! My name is Max Louis Miller. I am currently based in New York where I bartend/manage front-of-house hospitality operations, study tax law, and serve as a Notary Public.

By night, I build things. I created this website as a personal archive and digital studio to house my visual art, audio experiments, the occasional musing, and other ongoing projects. Take a look around, and thanks for stopping by. Feel free to reach out as well.
